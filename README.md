## 📱 Mobile App Designs

### [Blue Kingdom - Mobile App](BKmobile.pdf)
**Client:** Saudi Ministry of Tourism / Saudi Diving Federation  
A comprehensive mobile application and website platform developed for the Red Sea diving community. Blue Kingdom serves as an intelligent catalogue for diving sites, featuring advanced navigation capabilities, seamless dive computer and smartwatch integration, and detailed site information. The platform enables divers to discover, plan, and track their underwater adventures across the Red Sea's premier diving destinations.

### [Makani Mobile App](MKNMobile.pdf)
**Client:** Dubai Municipality  
An intuitive mobile and web-based navigation application designed for seamless city exploration in Dubai. The Makani App enables users to search locations using Makani numbers, place names, and plot numbers, with intelligent routing based on current or selected locations. Features include precise floor plan digitalization with realistic 2D/3D building models, incorporating architectural details such as rooms, corridors, stairs, elevators, and landmarks for enhanced indoor navigation.

### [Emirates Post Mobile App](EmiratesPost.pdf)
**Client:** Emirates Post Group  
A comprehensive mobile and web application developed for Emirates Post Group, delivering a full suite of postal services to users. The platform streamlines access to various postal department services, enabling customers to manage mail, parcels, tracking, and other postal operations efficiently through an intuitive digital interface.

## 🌐 Website Designs

### [Bairaq Al Namoos (BAQL)](BAQL.pdf)
**Client:** Dubai Camel Racing Club (Under H.H. Sheikh Hamdan)  
A sophisticated website developed for the prestigious Dubai Camel Racing Club, operating under the patronage of H.H. Sheikh Hamdan. The platform showcases the rich heritage of camel racing while providing modern digital solutions for race management, event information, and community engagement.

### [Young Arab Leaders (YAL)](YAL.pdf)
**Client:** NGO under H.H. Sheikh Mohammed  
A dynamic web platform for Young Arab Leaders, an influential NGO under the supervision of H.H. Sheikh Mohammed. YAL has redefined its mission to enhance member benefits while driving positive change across the Arab region. The platform features comprehensive board member profiles highlighting impactful achievements and societal contributions. The YAL Board of Directors leads strategic planning, ensures financial sustainability, and implements programs across the region, bringing international best practices to strengthen the organization's governance framework.

### [Blue Kingdom - Web Platform](BKWeb.pdf)
**Client:** Saudi Ministry of Tourism / Saudi Diving Federation  
The companion web platform to the Blue Kingdom mobile application, offering desktop access to the comprehensive diving site catalogue. The platform provides enhanced visualization and planning capabilities for dive operators, tourism agencies, and diving enthusiasts exploring Red Sea diving opportunities.

### [ADEK Art E-Commerce Platform](ADEK.pdf)
**Client:** Ministry of Education, UAE  
A comprehensive art e-commerce platform that facilitates the buying and selling of artworks. This digital marketplace connects artists, art enthusiasts, and buyers, offering a curated selection of artworks with secure online purchasing. The platform serves various artists and art providers while delivering an exceptional user experience for art buyers and collectors exploring and acquiring artworks.

### [MAKANI Web Platform](MAKANI.pdf)
**Client:** Dubai Municipality  
The web-based counterpart to the Makani mobile application, providing comprehensive location services and navigation capabilities for Dubai. The platform enables users to leverage Makani's unique addressing system through desktop access, with advanced mapping and route planning features.

### [Travvise Gamification Platform](Travvise.pdf)
**Client:** STS / RTA Dubai  
An innovative gamification platform developed for road safety awareness initiatives proposed by RTA Dubai. Travvise transforms road safety education into an engaging, interactive experience, encouraging users to adopt safer driving behaviors through game mechanics, challenges, and rewards.

### [TTE Corporate Website](TTE.pdf)
**Client:** TTE (Algurg Group Subsidiary)  
A professional corporate website for TTE, a subsidiary of the prestigious Algurg Group. The platform showcases the company's services, expertise, and market position while maintaining the high standards of corporate communication expected from Algurg Group entities.
